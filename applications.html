Machine Learning in the Real World

Machine learning is used in research, businesses, and everyday technology. It can reveal patterns that are difficult to identify manually.

Healthcare

ML can help analyze medical images, estimate health risks, and support clinical research. Human oversight remains essential.

Drug Discovery

Researchers use molecular properties and experimental data to prioritize promising compounds and investigate possible drug effects.

Recommendation Systems

Streaming and shopping services can use patterns in user activity to recommend movies, music, and products.

Fraud Detection

Financial institutions use ML to identify unusual transaction patterns that may warrant further review.

Responsible Use of ML

Models can make mistakes and reflect biases in their training data. Privacy, fairness, transparency, and careful evaluation are important when using machine learning, especially in healthcare.
